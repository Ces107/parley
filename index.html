Rehearsal is a complete scenario for trying the game out. Class is a different one, kept unseen for the night itself.

Before class
  1. Open the game on the big screen. The names go in there; each player then scans their own code.
  2. Pencils and scrap paper for everyone. The Council also wants two coins each.
  3. Printing is optional. Rehearsal: minister’s sheet · case notebook and exhibits.
  4. Class: minister’s sheet · case notebook and exhibits. The exhibits give the case away: print them without reading and hand them out face down.
  5. Keys on the big screen: → next · space starts and stops the clock · F full screen · 1 to 4 buzz, in The Panel.
